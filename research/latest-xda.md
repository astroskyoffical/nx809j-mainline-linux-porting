Forum posts that mention RM10 Pro, 11S Pro, Astra, Z70U, or Z80U are not interchangeable evidence for the RedMagic 11 Pro. In particular, an RM10 fingerprint success and a Z70U/NX733J Firehose recovery request do not establish NX809J behavior. The project’s own current fastboot capture remains the authority for its phone; Android’s `ro.boot.flash.locked` or green-state property in a forum post should not override fastboot’s reported unlock state.
