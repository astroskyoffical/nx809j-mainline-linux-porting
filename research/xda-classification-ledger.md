### Unresolved distinctions

- The posts do not prove that every byte in persist, vm-persist, ztecfg, efisp, devinfo, keymaster, keystore, FRP or the secure-state partitions is unique per handset. Some are described as identity-bearing, some as mutable boot/security state, and some only as “do not replace.” Keep them protected while separating those meanings and marking unverified contents.
- keymaster and keystore should not be treated as interchangeable names: Dimachi lists both together but gives no dump-level evidence that they store the same keys or biometrics. efisp and ztecfg also have unresolved roles across the cited advice.
